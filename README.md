# CLLRS Supplementary Material

This repository contains supplementary material for **Compact Lattice-Based Linkable Ring Signatures for Decentralized Anonymous Voting**.

The folders are named after the manuscript sections that use their contents. They provide the detailed lemma proofs, finite-profile parameter bounds, and operation-budget calculations supporting the construction and its analysis.

## Numbering and navigation

Lemma, definition, theorem, algorithm, and manuscript-section numbers follow the main paper. Each supplementary PDF has its own equation numbering.

The general key-norm bound, response-norm bound, and sampling-error budget appear in Sections A and D of the [proof document](%E2%85%A4%5C%E2%85%A5/%E2%85%A4%5C%E2%85%A5.pdf). The other two documents use those derivations when analysing the finite profiles and their resource budgets.
