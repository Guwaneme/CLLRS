# Sharper Bounds for the Finite Profiles

This folder contains the parameter-bound derivations supporting **Section V, Setup**, and **Section VII-B1, System Parameters**, of the main paper. This material was originally Appendix I.

## Files

- [Read the PDF](%E2%85%A4%5C%E2%85%A6.pdf).
- [View the LaTeX source](%E2%85%A4%5C%E2%85%A6.tex).

## Contents

The document derives sharper bounds for the same key and response distributions used in the construction:

- A key-norm rejection bound from discrete-Gaussian exponential moments.
- A response-norm tail bound for the integer-Gaussian distribution.
- An invertibility lower bound from finite Fourier analysis and a union bound over the roots.
- Coset-enumeration constants and the fixed rational values used for the three parameter profiles.
- The resulting norm-tail and cutoff inequalities for those profiles.

## Where the manuscript uses this material

- **Section V, Setup; footnote 1:** the tighter alternatives to the sufficient general norm choices.
- **Section VII-B1, System Parameters; footnote 4:** the sharper bounds supporting the displayed norm and cutoff choices.

## Companion derivations and numbering

The general key- and response-norm choices are derived in Sections A and D of the [proof document for Sections V and VI](../%E2%85%A4%5C%E2%85%A5/%E2%85%A4%5C%E2%85%A5.pdf). They are Equations (1) and (4) in that companion PDF.

This PDF numbers its own displayed bounds separately: Equation (1) is the sharper key-norm rejection bound, Equation (2) is the response-norm tail bound, and Equation (3) is the invertibility lower bound.

The resulting bounds are used in the [finite-budget calculations](../%E2%85%A6/%E2%85%A6.pdf).
